Duke Food Points Tracker

Jacob Baker-Spitzer | Class of 2030

The first semester of my freshman year, I found myself with under four-hundred dollars with around 2 months to go. Effectively, I had burned seven-hundred dollars in a month and a half.

My goal is to make sure nobody experiences a shortage of points and a self-induced, forced starvation. This site that I have developed takes a user's inputted amount of dollars, and uses a variety of advanced metrics to plan how you can avoid going as broke as me.
